Hi, I’m Akintunde Dolapo Elisha (known across tech circles as BK / MAVIS / Klyvex). I am the Technical Site Director at High Q Solid Academy and a 400 Level Computer Science & Cybersecurity student at Ladoke Akintola University of Technology (LAUTECH), Nigeria.

I’ve always been driven by an relentless curiosity to understand how things work under the hood, taking them apart to build something far bigger. Beyond writing code, I am deeply passionate about giving back knowledge, mentoring others, and enlightening people on cyber awareness and the dangers of technology misuse. I believe computing power is a tool for empowerment, but only when accompanied by digital literacy and ethical security practices.
