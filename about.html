THE PERSON & MINDSET

ABOUT ME

Akintunde Dolapo Elisha · Nicknamed BK · Known as MAVIS

Hi, I’m Akintunde Dolapo Elisha, a 21-year-old Nigerian computer nerd, developer, gamer, cybersecurity student, and professional experimenter currently in 400 Level at Ladoke Akintola University of Technology (LAUTECH), Nigeria.

I’ve always been the kind of person who wants to know how things work — and, more importantly, what happens when I start taking them apart and rebuilding them. My journey in technology has grown from simply being fascinated by computers and games into building real-world software, experimenting with cybersecurity, exploring AI, and creating systems that solve problems I actually care about.

I primarily work around web development, cybersecurity, software engineering, automation, AI-assisted development, and experimental technology. I don’t pretend to know everything, and I’m not afraid to learn as I go. A lot of my projects come from curiosity: I get an idea, start researching, experiment with different technologies, use AI-assisted development where it helps, break things a few times, figure out what went wrong, and eventually turn the idea into something functional.

AI-Native Developer Mindset

One thing that defines me as a developer is that I’m AI-native. I use modern AI tools as part of my development workflow — not as a replacement for curiosity or problem-solving, but as another tool in the toolbox. I’m comfortable learning unfamiliar technologies through experimentation and building things before I necessarily know every part of the technology perfectly. That approach has allowed me to move between different types of projects and learn considerably faster than I otherwise would.

Outside of larger builds, I’m also a gamer, and gaming has influenced a lot of the way I think about technology. I’m interested in the intersection between games, software, AI, and creative technology, and I enjoy exploring what happens when those worlds overlap.

At the same time, I’m still learning. I don’t claim expertise in technologies I haven’t genuinely worked with, and I believe there’s value in being able to say “I don’t know this yet” and then going out to figure it out.

Beyond the technical side, I’m naturally kind-hearted, curious, slightly chaotic in the way only a computer nerd can be, and genuinely fascinated by technology. I like building things, solving weird problems, gaming, experimenting with computers, and occasionally going down a three-hour rabbit hole because I wanted to understand one tiny thing.

My Long-Term Goal

"Keep building bigger, smarter, and more meaningful things."

I’m interested in the future of AI, gaming, cybersecurity, software, and creative technology, and I’m exploring what could eventually grow into something much bigger of my own.

I’m Akintunde Dolapo Elisha — developer, cybersecurity student, gamer, computer nerd, and builder of things that probably started as “wait… can I actually make this?”
