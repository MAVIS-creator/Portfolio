Academic & Institutional Infrastructure

Blockchain-Secured Attendance System

BLOCKCHAIN SECURITY

A tamper-evident, cryptographic attendance verification infrastructure engineered to prevent proxy signing and attendance fraud. Integrates SHA-256 hash chaining, device fingerprinting, geofenced GPS verification, and Web3 anchoring.

  • ✓
    SHA-256 Cryptographic Chain: Each attendance record points to the SHA-256 hash of the previous record.
  • ✓
    Device & Location Anchoring: Captures unique hardware fingerprints and validates location boundaries.
  • ✓
    Web3 & Immutable Audit: Ensures records cannot be modified retroactively without breaking the chain.
